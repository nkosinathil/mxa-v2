Transcription Insights update

This build adds a dedicated Disabled transcription state.

What changed:
- Audio records now persist transcription_status and transcription_reason in SQLite.
- The parser marks audio as Disabled when transcription was turned off during processing.
- The Insights tab now shows:
  - Transcribed
  - Skipped
  - Disabled
- The Search tab transcription filter now includes Disabled.
- Clicking Disabled in Insights opens Search filtered to audio items where transcription was disabled.

Notes:
- Existing databases created before this change may not have transcription_status populated.
- New processing runs will save the explicit status correctly.
